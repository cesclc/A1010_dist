## codeSample/ex7_solution02.txt - What is the data telling us
  
  Typical features you should see in the image:

  1. Spiral/comma-shaped cloud pattern - characteristic of rotating low-pressure systems
  2. Dense cloud bands - associated with frontal boundaries (warm front and cold front)
  3. Dry slot - a clear/darker area wrapping around the backside of the low pressure center
  4. Various cloud types:
    - Bright white: Dense clouds (cumulonimbus, thick stratocumulus)
    - Wispy/thin white: High cirrus clouds ahead of the system
    - Darker areas: Cloud-free or thin cloud regions

  Key characteristics of mid-latitude cyclones:
  - Large-scale (hundreds to thousands of km across)
  - Counter-clockwise rotation in Northern Hemisphere
  - Bring widespread precipitation, wind, and temperature changes
  - Form along the jet stream in middle latitudes (30-60°N)
