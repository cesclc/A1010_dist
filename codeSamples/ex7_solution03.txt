## codeSample/ex7_solution02.txt - ABI data format and content
  
ABI stands for Advanced Baseline Imager - it's the primary instrument on NOAA's GOES-R series satellites (GOES-16, GOES-17, GOES-18).

  About ABI

  Satellite: GOES-16 (launched 2016, operational over Eastern US/Atlantic)

  Capabilities:
  - 16 spectral bands (visible, near-IR, infrared)
  - High spatial resolution (500m - 2km)
  - Rapid scan rates (every 5-15 minutes for full disk)
  - Covers Americas, Atlantic, Eastern Pacific

  Band Examples:
  - Band 1 (0.47 µm): Blue - aerosols, visibility
  - Band 2 (0.64 µm): Red - clouds, vegetation
  - Band 3 (0.86 µm): Veggie - vegetation health
  - Bands 7-16: Infrared - temperature, moisture, atmospheric chemistry

  The Specific Demo Dataset

  Event: Mid-latitude cyclone over US, March 14, 2019

  What's included:
  filenames = get_us_midlatitude_cyclone_abi()
  # Downloads 16 NetCDF files - one per ABI spectral band

  Files contain:
  - Calibrated radiance/reflectance data
  - Geolocation information
  - Metadata (time, satellite position, etc.)
  - Covers CONUS (Continental US) sector

  Size: ~150-200 MB total

  Why this scene?
  - Classic weather system with clear features
  - Good for testing/learning satellite imagery
  - Shows various cloud types and atmospheric structures
  - Demonstrates multi-spectral analysis capabilities
